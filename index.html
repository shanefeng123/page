About Me

I am an Associate Lecturer at the School of EECS, University of Queensland (UQ). I obtained my PhD from UQ in 2026, advised by Associate Professor Guangdong Bai from CityUHK (previously UQ), Dr Sharif Abuadbba from CSIRO's Data61, and Dr Guowei Yang from UQ.

My research primarily focuses on the privacy and security of Machine Learning and AI systems, exploring potential vulnerabilities and developing robust defenses to protect these systems.
